# Data assumptions

Everything in `data/` is generated by `make world` and is gitignored. Only this
file is committed.

- All data is **synthetic**. No real names, phone numbers, NIDs or other
  personal data. All IDs are random strings.
- Synthetic results show that the method works. They do not prove real-world
  accuracy.
