# Combined-feature probe (not the Jachai model)

Generated by `make eval` (`python -m jachai.eval`). Synthetic data only.

A stock scikit-learn gradient-boosting classifier trained on the noisy, observable `case_label` for 70% of shops, scored on the other 30% of shops (never seen in training) against the hidden truth, next to the rules-only baseline on the same payments. It checks that the features together carry signal beyond the rules once no single feature gives the answer away. `same_flag_count`: the probe flags as many payments as the rules do (4,210 of 560,403 test payments).
